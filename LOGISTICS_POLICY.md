# Victorious MARKET: Logistics & Delivery Policy

**Effective Date:** [Insert Date]
**Region:** Uyo, Akwa Ibom State

This document outlines the official logistics policies governing Deliveries, Returns, Cash Handling, and Vendor Fulfillment on the Victorious MARKET platform. Because all logistics are centrally managed by Victorious MARKET (In-House Shipping), strict adherence to this policy is required to ensure a smooth ecosystem.

---

## 1. For Customers: Delivery & Return Terms

### 1.1 "Transfer on Delivery" & Payment
- **Preferred Payment:** Customers who opt to pay at the door are strongly encouraged to use our **"Pay via Paystack"** option. The dispatch rider will provide a secure QR code or payment link upon arrival.
- **Physical Cash:** While physical cash is accepted, digital transfer through the rider's app is the safest and fastest way to receive your items.
- **OTP Verification:** Deliveries will **not** be handed over without the customer providing the Delivery OTP sent to their app/phone.

### 1.2 Inspection & Returns at the Door
- **No Open-Box Policy:** Dispatch riders are strictly logistics agents. They are **not** permitted to wait for customers to unbox, test, or try on items at the door. 
- Once the OTP is provided and the package is handed over, the delivery is considered finalized.
- If an item is defective or incorrect, the customer must accept the package and immediately file a "Refund Request" through the Victorious MARKET app. 

### 1.3 Post-Delivery Refunds & Logistics
- **Non-Refundable Shipping:** If a refund is approved by the Admin, the customer will be refunded the full price of the product. However, the **initial delivery fee is strictly non-refundable**, as the logistics service was successfully rendered.
- **Return Drop-offs:** Victorious MARKET **does not dispatch riders to pick up returned items** from customer residences. It is the customer's responsibility to safely return the item to the designated Uyo Hub or mail it back to the Vendor before the refund funds are released to their wallet.

---

## 2. For Dispatch Riders: Compensation & Conduct

### 2.1 Performance-Based Compensation
- Riders earn their `deliveryman_charge` **only** upon the successful delivery of an order (when the status is updated to `delivered` in the app).
- Failed delivery attempts (e.g., customer unreachable, absent, or rejecting the package at the door) do not generate a delivery fee. 
- **Mandatory Pre-Call:** To avoid unpaid failed deliveries, riders must call the customer to confirm availability *before* leaving the hub/vendor.

### 2.2 Cash Remittance & Wallet Liabilities (`cash_in_hand`)
- Any physical cash collected by a rider (Cash on Delivery) is immediately recorded as a debt (`cash_in_hand`) owed to the platform.
- **24-Hour Remittance Rule:** Riders must remit all collected physical cash to the Victorious MARKET Hub within 24 hours.
- If a rider's `cash_in_hand` balance exceeds **₦20,000**, their account will be automatically suspended from receiving new orders until the balance is settled.
- To reduce cash liabilities, riders must always prioritize the "Paystack Transfer" option on their app when collecting payments at the door.

### 2.3 Failed Order Handover
- In the event of a canceled or failed delivery, the rider must return the package to the Vendor or the Uyo Hub by the end of their shift. Failure to return merchandise will result in the total value of the item being deducted from the rider's digital wallet (`current_balance`).

---

## 3. For Vendors: Handover & Fines

### 3.1 Order Readiness
- Because Victorious MARKET manages dispatch riders centrally, time is strictly monitored. When an order is marked as "Ready for Pickup" by a Vendor, the item must be fully packaged and available.
- **Wait-Time Penalty:** If a rider arrives and is forced to wait more than **15 minutes** because an item is out of stock, unpackaged, or the vendor is unavailable, Victorious MARKET reserves the right to cancel the order and deduct a penalty fee from the Vendor's digital `total_earning` wallet for wasting logistics resources.

### 3.2 Absorbing Refund Costs
- If a customer successfully applies for a post-delivery refund due to a defective or incorrect item, the full refund amount will be deducted from the Vendor's digital wallet. 
- Because Victorious MARKET successfully fulfilled the delivery to the customer on day one, the Vendor absorbs the loss of the initial delivery fee. Ensuring product quality prior to handover is strictly the Vendor's responsibility.
